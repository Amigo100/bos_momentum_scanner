## Quick Reference: The Core Brand Narrative

### The "Cyborg" Identity

Sterling Signals is positioned as **"Man + Machine"** — a human trader who coded emotion out of trading.

**Brand Voice:**
- Creator: "Alexander Sterling" — the architect who built the system
- Execution: Cold, systematic, emotionless code
- Tone: Confident but honest; technical but accessible; disciplined but not robotic

**Core Hook:**
> "I spent years coding the emotion out of my trading. Now I just execute what the code says."

### Target Audience

**Primary:** US Active Investors, Swing Traders, and Roth IRA Builders

**Psychographics:**
- Frustrated with "buy and hold" underperformance
- Want systematic approach but lack time/tools to build one
- Interested in momentum/growth over dividends
- Seeking alpha vs SPY/QQQ benchmark
- Weekly timeframe suits their lifestyle (not day trading)

## SECTION 2: Content Type Templates

### 2.1 Loss Acknowledgment ("Post-Mortem Card")

**Purpose:** Build trust by owning losses publicly. This is the #1 differentiator from typical fintwit.

**Template:**
```
❌ ${TICKER} — Stopped Out

Entry: $${ENTRY_PRICE}
Exit: $${EXIT_PRICE}
Loss: -${LOSS_PERCENT}%

The system protects capital so we live to fight another day.
No ego, just execution.

${OPTIONAL_LESSON}
```

**Example:**
```
❌ $SMCI — Stopped Out

Entry: $42.50
Exit: $34.85
Loss: -18%

The system protects capital so we live to fight another day.
No ego, just execution.

Lesson: Theme momentum faded faster than institutional accumulation could support. The forensic audit was borderline—we took the shot, it didn't work. On to the next.
```

**Rules:**
- Post IMMEDIATELY when stop is hit
- Never delete losing trade posts
- Always frame positively (system working as designed)
- Optional: Add brief lesson learned
