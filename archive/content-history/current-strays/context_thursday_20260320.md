### The Five Stages (public-facing descriptions only)

**Stage 1: Momentum Confirmation**
The system scans approximately 1,300 US-listed stocks each Friday after market close. It identifies stocks where the structural trend has reversed: where institutional capital is beginning to flow in and momentum is inflecting from negative to positive. This stage eliminates ~98% of the universe.

**Stage 2: Theme Quality Scoring**
Surviving stocks are grouped by their investment theme. Each theme is scored across four dimensions: catalyst density, momentum, crowding (lower is better), and runway. Themes are classified as PRIME, INVESTABLE, SELECTIVE, or AVOID. Only stocks in PRIME or INVESTABLE themes advance.

**Stage 3: Structural Force Alignment**
Stocks are mapped to seven macro structural forces: Defence Spending, Nuclear Renaissance, Biotech Capital Cycle, AI Infrastructure, Grid Modernisation, Cybersecurity Compliance, Financial Infrastructure. Stocks without structural force alignment are deprioritised.

**Stage 4: Forensic Research (Investment Gate)**
Each surviving stock undergoes deep fundamental research. Capital structure analysis (dilution, debt maturities, cash runway), catalyst verification (dates, specificity, proximity), red flag screening (insider selling, auditor changes, SEC filings), and competitive positioning. Immediate disqualifiers include: serial dilution, management departures before key milestones, active SEC investigations, or capital structure cliffs.

**Stage 5: Deep Due Diligence**
The final 2-3 stocks receive a comprehensive deal memo analysis: elevator pitch, forward maths (price targets from multiple valuation methods), bear case construction and stress-testing, risk monitoring triggers, entry timing, position sizing, and exit criteria. Only stocks where the bear case can be substantively addressed survive.

### Rejection Examples (compelling stories from March 14 scan)

**Example 1: DDD (Defence Additive Manufacturing)**
- Stage rejected: Deep Due Diligence (Stage 5)
- Theme: Defence Additive Manufacturing (INVESTABLE, 6.55 score)
- Wave strength: CONFIRMED (aligned to Defence Spending structural force)
- What looked good: NDAA FY2026 ban on foreign 3D printing for DoD. A&D segment grew 16% in 2025 with >20% guided for 2026. 23% short interest creating potential squeeze.
- What killed it: 45% share dilution in 2 years (130M to 189M fully diluted). $92M secured convertible notes with June 2028 put date. Revenue declined 12% YoY despite cost savings. $73M annual cash burn against $97M cash. A&D revenue represents only 9% of total revenue. Serial turnaround failure across a decade.
- Lesson: Momentum confirmation and theme alignment are necessary but not sufficient. Capital structure forensics protect capital from value traps masquerading as momentum plays.

**Example 2: LFMD (GLP-1 Obesity Telehealth)**
- Stage rejected: Review Gate (final quality check, between Stage 4 and 5)
- Theme: GLP-1 Obesity Telehealth (INVESTABLE, 7.45 score)
- What looked good: Cheapest pure-play GLP-1 telehealth at 0.8x revenue. Record 1,200/day patient sign-ups. Oral Wegovy launched Jan 2026.
- What killed it: HIMS announced Novo Nordisk partnership on March 9, demolishing competitive moat. Archetype score 2/7 (extreme system drift). Healthcare specialist funds (Deerfield) exiting 100%. Revenue restatement + active securities lawsuit. Zero insider buying during 75% stock price decline.
- Lesson: The system scores more than momentum and theme. It evaluates whether the stock fits the archetype of a historical winner. When the competitive landscape shifts materially between scan and deployment, the system adapts.

**Example 3: HIMX (Photonic Interconnect)**
- Stage rejected: Investment Gate (Stage 4)
- Theme: Photonic Interconnect (INVESTABLE, 6.85 score)
- What looked good: Potential involvement in TSMC co-packaged optics supply chain. Significant revenue expansion if confirmed.
- What killed it: Stock surged 35% on speculation. Price at $9.84 above all analyst targets. Entry timing classified as CHASING. The maths failed at current price.
- Lesson: The system does not chase. If the maths do not work at the current price, the stock goes to the watchlist regardless of how compelling the narrative is. Discipline over FOMO.
