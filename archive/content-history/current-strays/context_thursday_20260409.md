## CONCEPT

A stock can have perfect momentum, a funded structural force, 20%+ short interest creating squeeze potential, and a genuine catalyst. If the capital structure is broken, none of it matters. This article uses the DDD rejection as a worked case study, then shows how the same lens saved us on other decisions and flagged risk in current positions.

## CAPITAL STRUCTURE RED FLAGS (framework for the article)

The W14 Thursday post ("Balance Sheet Red Flag Checklist") gave subscribers the tool. This article shows the tool in action:

1. Share count trajectory: DDD +45% in 2 years. KARO +0% ever.
2. Financing patterns: DDD uses ATM and shelf offerings repeatedly. SVCO has not raised.
3. Management alignment: DDD rotating CEOs with no insider buying. KARO CEO owns 58%.
4. Cash runway vs burn: DDD burns cash and raises to survive. KARO generates $25M+ annual FCF.
5. Revenue trend: DDD -12% YoY despite tailwinds. KARO +20% with expanding margins.
