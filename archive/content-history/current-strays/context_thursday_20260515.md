The screening system processes every eligible US equity each session.

## THE TEACHING FRAMEWORK

### What makes the filter chain work

1. **Independence**: Each stage uses different data. Momentum detection
   does not know about themes. Thematic scoring does not see price
   action. Forensic analysis reviews everything with fresh eyes.

2. **Compounding selectivity**: A 97.7% rejection rate at Stage 2
   followed by 40% at Stage 3 followed by further elimination at
   Stages 4-5 means the final survivors have cleared orthogonal tests.

3. **Archetype fitness**: The system does not ask "is this a good
   company?" It asks "does this match the profile where our edge
   exists?" Sub-$500M. Structural force alignment. Confirmed momentum.
   Clean capital structure. Catalyst density. Management quality.

4. **Anti-conviction drift**: The CRO stage exists specifically to
   catch cases where analysis time creates attachment. DLO scored
   beautifully on fundamentals. The system said no because the
   archetype fit was 2/7.

### Why 99.8% rejection is the point

The system's edge comes from extreme selectivity, not broad coverage.
Thirteen positions. Average market cap at entry: $412M. Five structural
forces. The alpha exists in the gap between what the system finds and
what institutional research economics allow large funds to cover.
