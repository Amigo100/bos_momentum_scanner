99% of stock screeners filter on what already happened. Revenue growth last quarter. Earnings beats. 52-week high proximity.

Our system filters on what is changing. Structural trend reversals. Institutional accumulation building. Capital flowing into forces that will drive the next 12 to 18 months of returns.

Backward-looking screens find stocks that already moved. Forward-looking screens find stocks that are about to. The difference is where roughly 99% of the alpha sits.

1,300 tickers enter our screening process each Friday. Fewer than five typically survive. The filter chain is designed to be selective because capital preservation matters more than signal volume.
