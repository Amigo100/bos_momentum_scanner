25,967 US stocks from 1926 to 2016. Hendrik Bessembinder at Arizona State studied every one. The finding: just 4% generated all net wealth creation above Treasury bills.

The other 96% collectively matched or underperformed one-month T-bills over their entire lifetimes.

This is why systematic screening matters. Finding the 4% is not about conviction or instinct. It is about building a process that eliminates the 96% before capital is deployed. Our system screened 1,279 tickers last cycle. Three survived. That is a 99.8% rejection rate.

The screening is the product.

Source: Bessembinder (2018), Journal of Financial Economics, 129(3), 440-457.
