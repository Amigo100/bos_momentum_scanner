Between 2009 and 2024, stocks that returned 1,000% or more shared three characteristics at the point of entry: small market capitalisation, free cash flow yield above the sector median, and purchase near a 12-month low after a significant drawdown.

Past earnings growth did not predict future multibagger returns. Not EPS growth. Not revenue growth.

The common thread was buying structural quality at cyclical or sentiment-driven lows. This is why our screening process filters for momentum inflections rather than momentum continuations. The system targets stocks where the direction is changing, not stocks where the direction already changed.
