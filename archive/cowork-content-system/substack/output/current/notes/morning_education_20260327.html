8.7% annualised alpha. That is the average outperformance Lakonishok and Lee found when three or more corporate insiders bought shares within the same 90-day window.

The effect is strongest at depressed prices. Cluster buying near 52-week lows produced even wider spreads in subsequent research covering 2000 to 2023. Single insider purchases barely moved the needle. Clusters did.

The logic is simple. One insider might buy for tax planning, compensation optics, or routine allocation. Three insiders buying with their own capital within the same quarter signals a shared internal view that the market is mispricing the company. They are seeing something the tape has not absorbed yet.

Our screening process tracks insider activity during the forensic analysis stage. It is one of several signals that distinguish a momentum inflection backed by conviction from one backed by nothing.
