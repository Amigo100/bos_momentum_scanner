42% of our portfolio by position count sits in one structural force: AI Infrastructure. Five positions. $AMD, $NVDA, $BAND, $SVCO, $HIVE.

That looks like concentration risk. It is not.

Each company operates in a different layer of the stack. Chips. Voice infrastructure. EDA tools. Data centre compute. No two share the same revenue driver or customer base. A slowdown in hyperscaler capex hits $NVDA directly but barely touches $BAND, which sells voice minutes to enterprises.

The screening system does not target sectors. It targets structural forces and then diversifies across the value chain within each force. Same thesis. Different business models. Different risk profiles.

When we say "five positions in AI Infrastructure," what we mean is five independent bets on different layers of the same capital cycle.
