01 — Market Context

February Closes Red, Rotation Fatigue Sets In

Markets closed February on a sour note as a hotter-than-expected PPI print rattled investors on Friday. The S&P 500 finished the month down 0.9%, the Nasdaq dropped 3.4%, and the Dow was the lone survivor at +0.2%. Small caps gave back ground too, with the Russell 2000 sliding on Friday. The VIX spiked to 19.86, touching 21.74 intraday — its highest level since the tariff turbulence earlier this month.

The big story remains rotation fatigue. The Mag 7 premium has compressed to pre-pandemic levels. Value sectors like materials, energy, and utilities have outperformed YTD, but valuations there are now stretching too. One analyst called it: the rotation may be peaking. Meanwhile, the 10-year yield dropped below 4.0% as safe-haven buying intensified alongside fresh Iran uncertainty and tariff escalation headlines.
