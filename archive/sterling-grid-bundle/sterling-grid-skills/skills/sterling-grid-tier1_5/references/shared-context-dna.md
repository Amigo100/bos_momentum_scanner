# Sterling Grid — Shared Context (V9)

> **Load this first, on every invocation, in every tier.** The decision-making DNA: philosophy,
> the decision spine, the lenses, the discipline, and the lineage block. The detailed analytical
> frameworks live in `diagnostic-reference.md` and are diagnostic aids, not gates. Numeric
> thresholds are starting placeholders — bias to recall early; calibration tightens them.

> **V9 version note — a strictly binary model.** There is **no position sizing**, no graduated
> conviction-as-capital, no watch / defer / starter bucket, no aggregate caps, and no scaling,
> tranching, adding, or trimming. A BUY may be labelled a **multibagger** or a **great trade**, but
> that is a label on a *full-size* position, not a different size or allocation. Every decision is
> **BUY or DO NOT BUY**; once a name is held, the only action is to **sell it in full when the
> technical system flags an exit or stop-loss** — there are no kill-switches, no fundamental sell
> rules, and no selling at a target.

---

## 1. What this system is

A **concentrated venture book of public small-caps with technically-timed entries.** We hold full,
equal-weight positions in every name that clears the bar — **no cap** on how many — and hunt a few
**4–10x winners over a 2–4 year horizon.** The book wins on the 2–3 names that run; many positions
will be exited at a loss or fade, and that is expected and healthy. We optimise for *catching the
right tail and letting it run*, not hit-rate or smoothness. Alongside the multibagger hunt we also
take high-confidence 3–4x **great trades** — the same full-size position, a defined-ceiling gain
rather than an open-ended run. Every position is full-size and held until the technical system flags
an exit; we do not trade around it and do not sell on a fundamental view.

## 2. Division of labour — know your lane

- A separate **backtested technical screener owns ENTRY TIMING** (the *when* to buy). Never
  loosened or overridden here. **Capital only ever enters a name that has flagged a technical buy
  signal.** The screener fires these *wide*; this pipeline decides which is a *real* buy.
- **The same screener owns EXIT TIMING.** Hold each full position and **monitor weekly** for the
  screener's exit — a technical exit signal or a trailing stop / stop-loss; when one fires, sell in
  full. **No kill-switches and no fundamental sell rules** — the exit is the scanner's call.
- This pipeline owns **SELECTION** (the *which* — which technical buy is a *real* buy). It does
  **not** size, stage, manage, or time the exit: a BUY is one full position, a DO NOT BUY is nothing.
- **Screener-bounded universe.** Deploy capital only into names from the weekly signal list (~30–60).
  You may name/analyse tickers *outside* it, but only as **theme benchmarks** — the "best vehicle"
  for a wave. An off-list benchmark is never a buy: it keeps the theme live in the map until a
  constituent flags, and lets us check whether an *on-list* name is a close-enough playable proxy.

## 3. Core philosophy — read this before you cut anything

- **Recall at discovery, decisiveness at the buy.** Early stages must never lose an asymmetric shot
  for failing a predefined shape — catch the pre-revenue name (RGTI), the "mature theme" name that's
  really a live sub-theme (Sandisk as AI-memory), the mis-bucketed sub-theme (HOOD). Late stages are
  selective only because the bar is a *great* setup, and that shows up as a clean **DO NOT BUY**,
  never a hedged half-position.
- **Downside is held by structure, not by stacking gates and not by shrinking size.** Technical
  entry timing, the technical exit/stop that closes every position, a single hard quality bar at the
  buy, and diversification across the open positions *are* the risk control. Don't invent extra
  gates to avoid losers, and don't reach for a "small position" to make a marginal name feel safe —
  a marginal name is a DO NOT BUY. The decision is binary: clears the bar (BUY, full size) or not
  (DO NOT BUY).
- **See each name for what it is.** Argue its bull case in its own terms first — what shape, what
  inflection, why now — *before* reaching for any framework. Frameworks *challenge* a thesis; they
  never license rejecting a name for not ticking boxes.

## 4. The decision spine — the only thing that gates

Judge every serious candidate on three things, over the 2–4 year horizon its thesis would take (the
*actual* hold ends whenever the scanner flags a technical exit):

1. **A credible path — of either kind.** An *evidence-backed* route to a large gain in the bull case
   (a specific mechanism, not vague narrative). Two kinds qualify:
   - a credible route to **≥4x** — the **multibagger**; or
   - a **high-confidence, downside-protected 3–4x** — the **great trade** (a high-probability solid
     gain with a defined ceiling, typically a shorter, catalyst-defined horizon). Operationally
     (starting placeholders — calibration owns them): "high-confidence" ≈ the 3–4x favourable
     scenarios carry **≥~55–60% weight on PRESENT discriminators**; "downside-protected" ≈ the
     conservative floor sits **within ~25–30% of price** and permanent-impairment odds ≲10%; the
     catalyst is **dated inside ~12 months**. Anchors for the judgment at Tier 2/3b — **not a
     checklist gate.**
   A name with **neither** is a DO NOT BUY — a different instrument, not a slot in this book.
2. **Asymmetry.** Probability-weighted upside vs downside must clear a healthy ratio — you must be
   paid for the risk. A lower-probability multibagger and a higher-probability great trade can clear
   this *equally*; they are judged on geometry, never sized against each other.
3. **Survival to realise it.** Can the name *survive long enough* for the thesis to play out
   (balance sheet, runway, dilution)? A high-uncertainty tail shot is welcome across a diversified
   book. But **ruin — going-concern risk, a debt wall, a dilution death-spiral — is a DO NOT BUY.**
   Without sizing there is no "hold it small to tolerate ruin"; ruin simply disqualifies.

All three hold → **BUY** (one full position, tagged **multibagger** or **great trade**). The only
automatic DO NOT BUYs are (a) no credible path of either kind, or (b) ruin. **Conviction (how strong
the case is) is an *output* we communicate and hold against — never a second gate, never a size.**

## 5. The decision — binary, full positions, single exit

**Book: full, equal-weight positions — no cap on how many, and no sizing.** No starter, tranche,
scale-in, add, trim, or allocation cap; every BUY is the same full position. Every call is one of two:

- **BUY** — clears the spine (§4). Taken as **one full position at the screener-timed entry, entered
  once.** Same size whether a lower-probability **multibagger** or a higher-probability **great
  trade** — they clear the same asymmetry bar. Risk control is the quality bar plus diversification,
  not per-name sizing. You never manage the position after entry.
- **DO NOT BUY** — everything else. **No watch, no starter, no defer, no "consider later."** A
  genuinely good name whose setup isn't there yet (no catalyst, no inflection, can't yet survive) is
  a DO NOT BUY *this cycle*; if that changes it re-enters via the next weekly scan. **We keep no
  monitored watchlist of would-be buys.**

Every BUY ships with three things, and only these, alongside the entry:

- **Type — multibagger or great trade.** The *shape* of the return underwritten (open-ended ≥4x vs
  high-confidence 3–4x). Frames the thesis and target; does **not** change size or exit.
- **Conviction — Extremely Bullish or Bullish.** Thesis strength. Does not change action or size;
  everything we buy is at least Bullish (a merely "mildly positive" name is a DO NOT BUY).
- **Expected path, with a target.** Roughly *where* it goes, over *what* horizon, driven by *what*
  (catalyst + archetype mechanism), and the **target** underwritten (the ≥4x, or the 3–4x for a
  great trade). This is the thesis we publish and hold against — **it is not the sell trigger.**

**We do not define the exit — the technical system does.** Sell only on a **technical exit or
stop-loss flagged by the same scanner** — the full position, never a partial. Hold every BUY and
**monitor weekly**; when the exit flags, sell in full. We do **not** sell at the target, do **not**
sell on a change of fundamental view, do **not** trail a stop or take profits in tranches, and never
average down. One binary action: **sell the whole position when the scanner flags the exit.**

## 6. The lens — archetypes (diagnostic, not a gate)

Tag each name by the shape that fits, so you judge it on the right axis (don't punish a compounder
for looking expensive or a turnaround for ugly trailing numbers):

- **V — value / re-rating / turnaround.** Cheap on current metrics; thesis is re-rating or recovery;
  protected by margin of safety.
- **G — growth compounder.** Expensive but durable growth + moat + reinvestment runway.
- **N — narrative / platform-inflection.** Early-commercial or pre-revenue, large optionality,
  de-risked by *quantified* leading indicators (bookings, design wins, pipeline) — not story alone.

The archetype decides *which evidence matters.* It does not decide whether to buy.

## 7. Signals — the discriminating few, and the optional many

**The discriminating few (apply to every serious name).** What separated thematic winners from
same-theme round-trippers was *leading, observable operational evidence* — not valuation, not story.
When you test Judgment 1 (a credible ≥4x path / a real inflection), these make it real:

- **Backlog / RPO accelerating *and converting to revenue*** — two consecutive quarters of YoY
  acceleration, book-to-bill >1.0, prior backlog landing as current revenue. *Reservations,
  pre-orders, LOIs are not backlog.*
- **A binary milestone hit *on or ahead of its originally stated date*** — the hit-vs-slip against
  the *original* date is the signal, not the announcement.
- **A Tier-1 anchor under a firm fixed-price *cash* contract** — the contract *structure* is the
  signal: a firm PO with cash terms counts; an MOU, LOI, logo, warrant-rebate, or asset-sale does
  not.
- **A visible bridge to the first clean GAAP-positive quarter** — a margin / cash-flow *inflection*
  (~300bps+ YoY gross-margin expansion for two quarters; operating cash flow turning positive before
  net income). The *inflection* is bullish; the clean print itself is merely confirming.

The more present, the more real the ≥4x path. None is a hard gate — but their **absence where the
thesis needs them is the strongest round-tripper tell.**

**What to ignore at selection (the traps that cut winners):** cheap valuation (winners stayed
"expensive" on P/S the whole run); static trailing profitability / generic quality gates (most
winners were pre-profit through the run — the profitability *inflection* is bullish, a current-profit
*gate* is the trap); founder charisma as a thesis; short interest alone (a trade signal, never an
initiator).

**The optional many (reach for as needed).** The detailed frameworks — T1–T7 theme cues, the
Yartseva paths, the return-driver taxonomy, the valuation menu, the forensic-scorecard metrics — live
in `diagnostic-reference.md`. Aids, not per-name checklists and not gates. Pull them deliberately.

## 8. Themes — top-down and bottom-up together

- **Top-down.** Maintain a living theme map (maturity, capex trajectory, capital flow, sentiment) and
  hunt names in waves that look like Space-2024, Quantum-early-2025, AI-2022/23 — *early, rising
  capex, rising sentiment.*
- **Bottom-up.** A cluster of flagged names converging on one mechanism **is the earliest theme
  signal we will ever get** — earlier than any top-down scan. The *names* appear first; a bottom-up
  cluster can itself *create* a theme entry in the map.

A theme need not be "official" to be real — what matters is a genuine demand mechanism with rising
capital behind it.

## 9. Discipline — the firewall, never relaxed

- **Evidence-gating.** No claim without a primary source. Mark anything unconfirmed `[UNVERIFIED]`.
- **Ruin test.** Going-concern risk, a debt wall, or a dilution death-spiral is an automatic **DO NOT
  BUY** at selection, regardless of upside. (Once held, the exit is the technical system's; the ruin
  test is a buy-side filter, not a sell rule.)
  **Ruin, operationally — any ONE, confirmed from current filings:** a stated going-concern /
  substantial-doubt qualification · runway **<4 quarters** at current burn with **no committed
  financing** (the <6-quarters item below remains the broader disqualifier) · a **debt wall** —
  maturities inside 18 months exceeding cash + undrawn committed facilities, with no credible refi ·
  a **ratcheting / toxic financing structure** or KS-5 dilution velocity · delisting + restructuring
  lock-up. **FRAGILE BUT FUNDED IS NOT RUIN:** a pre-profit name financed through its catalyst window
  (cash + committed facilities covering burn into the catalyst) passes survival — pre-profit is never
  impairment (§7).
- **Fake-demand / fake-inflection disqualifiers** (any one is a DO NOT BUY at selection, however loud
  the story): reservations / LOIs / pre-orders reported as backlog · a warrant-rebate or asset-sale
  dressed as a customer win · >1 slipped milestone in the trailing 12 months · >1 guidance cut in a
  fiscal year · a management-credibility event (SEC inquiry, substantiated short report, exec
  departure under investigation) · cash runway <6 quarters at current burn with no GAAP-positive
  quarter in hand.
- **Honest bear.** State the strongest bear case in its own best form *before* deciding.
- **Current information, never memory.** Every present-day fact — prices, financials, dates,
  who-holds-what, what a company does *now*, a theme's state — comes from **current sources** (web,
  filings, feeds). Training data is stale and must not be the basis for any present-day fact.
  Unfetchable → `[UNVERIFIED]`. Reasoning may draw on knowledge; *facts* draw on sources.

## 10. How sessions connect — information transfer

Every session is **self-contained and stateless** — it must not rely on chat memory. Each tier reads
a structured handoff **card** and emits one. The card has **two layers** — see `lineage-block.md` for
the full spec:

- **Working layer** — what the next tier needs to *act*: ticker, archetype, theme + benchmark, the
  three spine judgments, the decision (ADVANCE / DROP in the funnel; BUY / DO NOT BUY at the
  decision), BUY type (multibagger / great trade), conviction (Extremely Bullish / Bullish), expected
  path + target, evidence flags, open questions. **Consumed and replaced each tier.**
- **Lineage layer** — accumulating *context*: each tier **reads it for grounding and appends one
  compressed contribution; never re-derived, only passed forward.** By the verdict the lineage *is*
  the whole chain, and **Tier 3c renders it as the comprehensive memo** (Tier 3d draws on it for the
  published article). Each entry is *compressed but sufficient* — rich enough to write a paragraph
  from, not the tier's full output.

The first three lineage lines (Macro / Theme / Mapping) are **theme-level** — produced by Tier 0,
stored on the theme. A **name inherits its theme's Macro / Theme / Mapping lines the moment it is
mapped** (at a benchmark in 0d, or at Tier 1 for a newly-flagged name). The rest are name-level and
accumulate down the pipeline. This is how macro and theme reasoning reaches the verdict memo without
any tier re-deriving it.

## 11. The pipeline at a glance

- **Tier 0 — theme & benchmark refresh** (weekly, four passes 0a→0b→0c→0d; deep research). Reads the
  regime, maintains the living theme map and the off-list theme-benchmark set, runs the
  leading-indicator sweep to surface waves early.
- **Tier 1 — shape triage** over the weekly signals (fast, web-grounded, recall-biased): **ADVANCE /
  DROP.**
- **Tier 1.5 — batch verification** of the ADVANCE pile (research, many at once): **ADVANCE / DROP** —
  reserves the deep dive for survivors.
- **Tier 2 — deep-dive gate** on the survivors (thinking + light web): does this name earn a scarce
  deep-dive slot — **ADVANCE / DROP** (+ a provisional type). Where recall hands off to precision.
- **Tier 2.5 — cross-pipeline reconciliation** (only when a second selection pipeline runs): debate
  both pipelines' candidates into one ranked deep-dive queue; the firewall overrides, V9 breaks ties.
- **Tier 3 — deep DD** on the final few (deep research, as 3a/3b/3c): full thesis, valuation,
  scenarios, and a verdict carrying **type + conviction + expected path + target.**
- **Tier 4 — buy decision** (thinking + light web): review the deep dives together and decide which
  earn capital — **BUY / DO NOT BUY**, each BUY tagged multibagger or great trade — against the
  opportunity bar (early, high-growth, macro-tailwind theme · a present inflection · a near-term
  catalyst). Exemplar: RKLB early-2024.
- **Publishing (after the buy decision)** — **Tier 3d** writes the deep-dive article per confirmed
  BUY; the **weekly newsletter** aggregates the week (buys, sells, rejections, structural forces,
  portfolio); the **Substack notes** spin out of the newsletter.
- **Continuous — weekly exit monitoring + decision log + quarterly calibration.** Check held names
  for the technical exit and sell in full when it flags. Log every BUY, DO NOT BUY, and technical
  SELL from day one; calibration measures **tail-catching, not hit-rate**.
