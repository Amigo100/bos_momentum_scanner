## 1. Archetypes — V / G / N (the lens)

The lens that decides *which evidence matters* for a name, so you don't judge a compounder on a value yardstick or a turnaround on its ugly trailing print.

- **V — Value / turnaround / re-rating.** Cheap on current metrics; protection is margin-of-safety on intrinsic value; includes cyclical-trough names (cheap on trough metrics, re-rate on the cycle turn). *Examples: HOOD 2023, MARA 2023, NVDA 2016 at re-rating.*
- **G — Growth compounder.** Expensive on current metrics; durable high-return growth + an owned moat + a long reinvestment runway; clean balance sheet. Protection is growth durability, not a cheap entry. *Examples: PLTR ~$10, MELI 2014.*
- **N — Narrative / platform-inflection.** Early-commercial with small-but-real revenue, large optionality, de-risking through *quantified* leading indicators (RPO/backlog/design-wins/NRR) rather than lagging financials; higher variance. *Examples: RKLB ~$4, IONQ 2024.* A pre-commercial, narrative-only name with **no** quantified leading indicator (the RGTI-at-pure-hype shape) is **not** an N pass — it fails on absence of evidence, regardless of story.

## 2. Return Drivers — *how* the return is generated

A separate axis from the archetype (a name can be Driver-A + Archetype-G). Where the archetype says *how to judge*, the Driver says *how the return is made*. The operative split for the spine:

- **Revenue-generating Drivers** (earnings compounding, cyclical, asset-backed) — judge on the asset/earnings path; the asset–EBITDA alignment check (§9 below) applies.
- **Pre-revenue / catalyst Drivers** (binary milestone, pre-commercial inflection) — judge on runway, leading indicators, and the catalyst; asset-growth-exceeding-EBITDA is expected, not a flag.

*(The full lettered A–F taxonomy lives in the frozen library; for these purposes the revenue-vs-catalyst distinction above is what conditions the valuation method and the survival read.)*

**Per-industry focus — which of the four §7 signals are load-bearing vs structurally N/A.** The four
discriminators are industry-shaped; reading them flat produces false "absent" marks that look like
round-tripper tells when they are structural:

- **Defense / Aero / Semi / industrial:** backlog-converting + on-date milestone are load-bearing
  (book-to-bill is the native metric); firm cash contract is the anchor evidence.
- **SaaS / subscription:** there is no backlog — **ARR acceleration + NRR >110% stand in for
  backlog-converting**; the margin/CF inflection is load-bearing; "firm cash contract" reads as a
  multi-year committed contract (RPO), not a logo.
- **Hardware / devices:** firm POs / customer pre-payments + the margin inflection are load-bearing;
  reservations and pre-orders are still NOT backlog (§7 stands).
- **Biotech / pre-revenue:** the on-date milestone + runway-to-catalyst are load-bearing; backlog and
  margin inflection are **structurally N/A** — score them `absent (N/A: pre-revenue)`, never as a
  red-flag absence; the §7 tell is a *load-bearing* signal missing, and these were never load-bearing.

Rule: a structurally-N/A signal scores **`absent (N/A: <why>)`** on the 3a scorecard — distinct from
a true absence-where-the-thesis-needs-it, which remains the strongest round-tripper tell.

## 3. Comparable-Winner Pattern-Match

The antidote to rigid archetypes — it forces concrete named precedent instead of category-ticking, and it scales to shapes you haven't seen before.

- **Step 0 — Comparables.** Find 2–3 historical multibagger winners the name most resembles *at the comparable's pre-breakout moment.* Specific shared pattern features, not surface similarity — "both AI" is not a comparable; "both shipped first product to government in the prior 12 months, founder-CEO, <8 analysts" is.
- **Step 0b — Fail-lookalike.** Name one company that *looked* similar at the same stage but did **not** multibag, and articulate what was different. This is the rigour check.
- **Abuse guards:** comparables must be pre-breakout (NVDA-2016, not NVDA-2024), match on specific features, span shapes where appropriate, and be falsifiable. No credible comparable → the setup is too speculative.
- **Regime guard:** record the macro/liquidity regime in which each comparable's **peak multiple**
  printed. A peak achieved in an easing / risk-on regime is the **overshoot scenario's ceiling, not
  its base**, when today's Macro line reads tighter — state a regime-adjusted overshoot anchor rather
  than importing the raw peak. *(This adjusts the overshoot scenario's level/probability only — it is
  never a reason to clip the path or to DO NOT BUY; §7's expensive-trap rule stands, and Street highs
  remain a floor on the bull, never a cap.)*

## 4. The Six Universal Markers

Universal across all multibagger shapes; M2 is interpreted through the Step-0 comparables. Useful as a multibagger-quality read when a name warrants depth. **Decision heuristic:** mandatory M1 + M2 + M3 all pass, **and** 4+/6 total, **and** a credible comparable — else it's not a multibagger shape (which points it toward great-trade or pass, not an automatic kill).

1. **Pure-play theme exposure** *(mandatory)* — lives or dies by the theme; no backup story. *(PLTR=data fusion, RKLB=launch, IONQ=trapped-ion, MARA=BTC mining.)*
2. **Quality of revenue / revenue path** *(mandatory; comparable-anchored)* — Compounder: 25%+ rev growth + margin leverage. R&D-stage: early traction + ≥6 quarters runway + identifiable validation milestones. Cyclical: operating leverage + survives full-cycle drawdown + structural commodity tailwind. Re-rating: stable/improving revenue + clear multiple discount + a narrative shift.
3. **Theme tailwind acceleration** *(mandatory)* — capital flow, policy, adoption inflecting *now*, not "someday."
4. **Asymmetric optionality** — an adjacent product/market/platform not yet modelled by the Street. *(PLTR's AIP, RKLB's Neutron, HOOD's crypto/derivatives.)*
5. **Aligned, capable leadership** — founder ownership, supervoting, credible execution, skin in the game.
6. **Consensus underappreciation** — thin coverage, valuation reflects only the proven business, retail narrative not yet formed, or mis-categorised.

## 5. Theme-strength cues — T1–T7

Diagnostic cues for the theme read (the maturity/capex/flow/sentiment picture Tier 0 maintains). Not scored gates — prompts for *what to look at*:

- **T1** lifecycle maturity (S-curve × hype-cycle position → entry zone). **T2** end-demand momentum. **T3** capex / investment-cycle direction. **T4** capital cycle / supply position. **T5** policy / regulatory tailwind. **T6** hype-to-revenue gap (contracted named-counterparty demand at the top, narrative at the bottom). **T7** theme-capital inflection (is capital, coverage, mindshare *accelerating into* the theme with runway — the theme-birth detector).

*The operationalized versions live in `theme-intelligence.md`: S0–S4 staging (§1), the P1–P4
inflection precursors + lead-time priors (§2/§3), the bottleneck-migration method (§4), recognition
progression (§5).*

## 6. Theme-death triggers — TD-1…5

A "is this theme dying?" check (mostly not disarmable by a single name's merits):

- **TD-1** mechanism-class abandonment. **TD-2** regulatory disqualification. **TD-3** capital-flow reversal. **TD-4** a superior *investable* liquid proxy saturating the trade (strict three-condition test; never fires against the category leader itself). **TD-5** secondary-ticker crowding.

*The weekly TD sweep on HELD positions' themes is the `sterling-grid-theme-health` skill (after
Tier 0, ≤2 searches per theme, GREEN/AMBER/RED — informational only; exits stay the scanner's).*

## 7. Entry-zone & shape labels

- **Sweet Spot-V** — re-rating entry: an inflection meeting beaten-down sentiment.
- **Sweet Spot-M** — momentum/capital-inflection entry: accelerating fundamentals + capital flooding in, *expensive-OK* (the PLTR-2023 shape).
- **Elevated Expectations** — peak hype; caution. **Too-Early** — mechanism real but no inflection yet.
- **Monster-shape** — a structurally exceptional standalone with no thematic wave (judged on individual merit).
- **Value-capture grades** — Direct / Adjacent / Peripheral / R&D-stage; how much of the theme's economics the name actually captures.

## 8. The 8 Kill-Switches (+ carve-out)

The structured ruin/red-flag checklist. Run it when a name's profile raises the question — it feeds the **survival** judgment. KS-5 and the ruin test are firewall-level (hard); the rest are strong red-flags to weigh.

- **KS-1 Biotech dual-strike** — same-indication CRL + active Boxed Warning.
- **KS-2 Corroborated short report** — credible short thesis (falsifiable primary data) + a corroborating event (13F cut, 10b5-1, inquiry, restatement) in ~90 days.
- **KS-3 Accounting quality** — two of: negative equity / OTM converts >25% / recent restatement / bifurcated embedded derivatives.
- **KS-4 Operational reliability** — >20pp gap to best-in-class on the company-type reliability metric.
- **KS-5 Dilution velocity** *(pre-revenue; firewall)* — >15% YoY share growth (ex structured PIPEs).
- **KS-6 Superior liquid proxy** — a better vehicle dominates on liquidity / purity / NAV-transmission / scale.
- **KS-7 PE-sponsor exit clock** — >30% holder, fund near end-of-life / active distribution / strategic review.
- **KS-8 Anchor-customer distribution** — a >30%-of-revenue customer who is also a >5% holder reducing the stake into the rally.

**Carve-out test:** if a KS fires, ask whether it's *mechanistically* disarmable for this specific name (a real, evidenced reason it doesn't apply) — not merely argued away. A KS that can't be carved out stands.

## 9. Yartseva asset–EBITDA alignment

A fast multibagger-positive / red-flag diagnostic for revenue-generating names (skip for pre-revenue):

- EBITDA growth ≥ asset growth → **aligned** (multibagger-positive).
- Asset growth exceeds EBITDA by 1–10pp → **misaligned**; flag for investigation.
- Asset growth exceeds EBITDA by >10pp (and not pre-revenue) → **severe red flag** (a ~−23pp cohort return-shortfall predictor); needs a compelling, evidenced explanation or it's a survival concern.

## 10. The seven valuation methods

A menu — pick the 2–3 appropriate to the archetype/Driver to pin the conservative **downside floor** and the **upside path** (kept decoupled, per Tier 3):

historical multiple range · DCF (Gordon + exit multiple) · comparable companies · reverse DCF · catalyst-adjusted EV · sector-specific method · precedent M&A transactions — plus the 3-statement projection and bear-case stress tests. **V** leans on asset/normalised-earnings value; **G** on growth-DCF / reverse-DCF + the multiple path; **N** on dilution-adjusted milestone / TAM-capture.

## 11. Scenario-probability playbook (3b's anchor)

Probabilities are judgments, anchored here so they are consistent and calibratable — not formula
outputs. **Starting placeholders; K.1 revises with cohort evidence, one threshold at a time.**

**The base-rate prior** for a qualified small-cap inflection candidate (it cleared Tier 2, so it is
already not the average name):

```
bear 30 · base 40 · re-rate 20 · overshoot 10      (always sums to 100)
```

**Shift rules — apply in writing, one at a time, each tied to named evidence:**
- Each §7 discriminator **present where the thesis needs it**: move ~5–10 pts bear/base → re-rate.
- The **humility release** (floor within ~15% of price + discriminators present + theme early/rising
  on the Tier-0 map): move up to 10 pts base → re-rate/overshoot.
- 3a-T multiple-ceiling reads **early/accelerating**: overshoot may carry 15–20. Reads
  **maturing / already-priced / crowding**: cap overshoot ≤5 and fold the residual into re-rate.
- Any discriminator **absent where the thesis needs it**: bear +10–15; cap overshoot ≤5.
- Decisive bear is an **unresolved binary**: cap re-rate + overshoot at ~35 combined until resolved.
- Market **priced for failure while the discriminators are present**: weight INTO the dislocation
  (that gap is the edge) — never toward the market-implied number for its own sake.

State each departure from the prior and the evidence that earned it; the written shifts are what K.1
audits against outcomes.
