### Why This Calendar

**Tuesday Deep Dive** — peak engagement day for financial content. Readers
want actionable analysis early in the week.

**Wednesday Sector Watch** — midweek theme analysis connects portfolio
positions to macro flows. Bridges Tuesday's ticker focus and Thursday's
educational angle.

**Thursday The Edge** — educational content tied to the portfolio. "Why 99%
of stocks fail our screen" type pieces. Positions Sterling Signals as a
thinking investor, not just a signal service.

**Friday Investor Lessons** — weekend-preview educational content. Investors
plan over the weekend — giving them a case study or principle on Friday
means it's fresh when they're reviewing positions Saturday morning. This
also catches the "weekend reading" audience.

**Saturday Tools & Tech** — pairs with the newsletter. Readers who just
got the weekly screening want to know how to do their own analysis. Tools
posts directly serve that need and position Sterling Signals as a resource,
not just a newsletter. Also strong for SEO and evergreen traffic.

## New Series: Investor Lessons

### What It Covers (rotate through these subcategories)

| Subcategory | Examples | Frequency |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **Case Studies** | Enron collapse, Tesla's short squeeze, GameStop, Nvidia's pivot from gaming to AI | Monthly |
| **Legendary Investors** | Buffett's value principles, Druckenmiller's macro approach, Peter Lynch's "invest in what you know", Cathie Wood's conviction investing | Monthly |
| **Investing Principles** | Position sizing, stop losses, risk/reward ratios, sector rotation, mean reversion, momentum vs value | Bi-weekly |
| **Market Mechanics** | How short squeezes work, 13F filing analysis, options flow reading, dark pool activity | Monthly |
| **Behavioural Finance** | Loss aversion, anchoring bias, FOMO psychology, recency bias, survivorship bias | Monthly |

### Key Rule: Always Connect to Sterling Signals

Every Investor Lessons post MUST connect the principle to our portfolio,
system, or recent trades. Abstract lessons without portfolio connection
feel generic. Examples:

- "Druckenmiller held 100% of his fund in one trade. We hold 5 positions
  max. Here's why concentration works — and where the line is."
- "Stop losses saved us on $VNET. Here's the math of why systematic exits
  beat 'I'll decide later.'"
- "$LUNR +140% in 67 days. Peter Lynch would call this a 'stalwart turning
  fast grower.' Here's his framework applied to our portfolio."

### Sequential Prompts (3 stages)

**Prompt 1 — DISCOVER TOPIC:**
Priority order for topic selection:
1. PORTFOLIO CONNECTION — a recent trade that perfectly illustrates a principle
2. MARKET EVENT — something in the news that connects to a timeless lesson
3. READER RELEVANCE — a question or misconception common among our audience
4. CLASSIC STUDY — a legendary investor or famous case study (last resort —
   these are excellent but harder to connect to our live portfolio)

Present 2-3 candidates with portfolio connection angle. Recommend one.

**Prompt 2 — RESEARCH:**
Web search for:
- The original source (Buffett's letter, the actual study, the SEC filing)
- Specific numbers: dates, returns, drawdowns, position sizes
- Counter-arguments: when does this principle fail?
- Which portfolio position or scanner result illustrates this?
- A surprising stat that contradicts common wisdom

**Prompt 3 — WRITE:**
Article (800-1,200 words) + companion note.

Structure:
1. Hook — the surprising number or counter-intuitive claim
2. The Story — the case study, investor, or principle explained accessibly
3. The Evidence — specific numbers, dates, returns. Show the data.
4. In Our Portfolio — REQUIRED. Name the ticker, entry, outcome. "Here's
   how this plays out in real time with our $RCAT position."
5. The Exception — intellectual honesty. When does this fail?
6. The Takeaway — one concrete, actionable thing the reader can do this week
7. Footer

Title: `Investor Lessons: [Specific, Surprising Topic]`

Examples:
- "Investor Lessons: Druckenmiller's Biggest Bet Was 100% of His Fund"
- "Investor Lessons: The Stop Loss That Saved Our $VNET Trade"
- "Investor Lessons: Why 90% of 'Cheap' Stocks Stay Cheap"
- "Investor Lessons: The 13F Filing That Predicted NVDA's Rally"

## New Series: Tools & Tech

### What It Covers (rotate through these subcategories)

| Subcategory | Examples | Frequency |
|-------------|----------|-----------|
| **Screeners & Scanners** | Finviz, TradingView screener, EDGAR full-text search, SEC filing tools | Monthly |
| **Charting Platforms** | TradingView setup guide, key indicators to configure, template sharing | Monthly |
| **Data & Research** | Koyfin, Simply Wall St, Macrotrends, FRED economic data, Quiver Quant | Bi-weekly |
| **Portfolio Management** | Position sizing calculators, stop loss tools, correlation matrices, Brokerage features | Monthly |
| **AI & Automation** | Using Claude for earnings analysis, ChatGPT for 10-K reading, AI screener tools | Monthly |
| **Free vs Paid** | "The free tools that replace $500/year subscriptions" — audience loves these | Quarterly |

### Key Rule: Show, Don't Just List

Every Tools & Tech post shows the tool IN ACTION on a real ticker from
our portfolio or watchlist. Not "here are 5 screeners" — instead "I ran
$ASTS through these 3 tools. Here's what each found and what I'd miss
without them."

### Sequential Prompts (2 stages)

**Prompt 1 — RESEARCH & TEST:**
Web search for the tool(s) being covered:
- Official site, pricing, free tier limitations
- What it does that alternatives don't
- Specific workflow: "Open [tool] → enter [ticker] → look for [metric]"
- Run the tool on 1-2 portfolio tickers — what does it surface?
- Limitations and gotchas
- Screenshots or screen descriptions for the article

**Prompt 2 — WRITE:**
Article (600-1,000 words) + companion note.

Structure:
1. The Problem — what analysis challenge does this solve?
2. The Tool — what it is, free/paid, who it's for
3. How We Use It — specific workflow with a portfolio ticker as example.
   "I plugged $LUNR into [tool] and here's what it showed..."
4. What It Found — the actual output, interpreted. Specific numbers.
5. Limitations — what it misses, what it gets wrong, when to use something else
6. Setup Guide — 3-5 steps to get started. Actionable.
7. Footer

Title: `Tools & Tech: [Tool Name] — [What It Does in One Line]`

Examples:
- "Tools & Tech: Finviz — The Free Screener That Finds Our Signals"
- "Tools & Tech: Koyfin — Institutional-Grade Charts for $0"
- "Tools & Tech: Using Claude to Read 10-Ks in 5 Minutes"
- "Tools & Tech: The TradingView Setup Every Momentum Trader Needs"
- "Tools & Tech: 5 Free Tools That Replace a $500/Year Bloomberg Terminal"
