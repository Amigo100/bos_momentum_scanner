### 1. Macro — the regime it rides

**Structural force: AI Infrastructure.** The position sits on the same multi-year capital wave that funds the entire AI build-out, but it enters that wave one layer *below* the obvious compute names — at the **design and process-development tooling** every advanced chip must pass through before it is manufactured. The regime carried down the lineage is intact and easing-into-risk: AI-infrastructure capex is structurally rising, and the tooling layer is among its most *defensive* expressions because EDA spend runs at a stable **~2% of semiconductor revenue regardless of the cycle**. The tailwind here is not "AI hype" — it is the harder, slower fact that as chips get more complex and process development gets more expensive, the simulation and design-automation spend *per chip* rises, and AI is now being turned back on that workflow itself (digital-twin process development). A rising-capex, rising-mindshare wave with a long runway — the shape the book hunts.

### 2. Theme — Semiconductor Design Software (EDA): why it qualifies, its stage, its recognition

**Theme: Semiconductor Design Software (EDA), as an AI-Infrastructure sub-theme.** EDA qualifies as a genuine, capital-backed demand mechanism, not a narrative. The terminal economics are proven and brutal in the incumbents' favour: Synopsys and Cadence trade at **11-16x revenue** (SNPS ~11.2x EV/Rev, ~15.2x P/S; CDNS 18.6x P/S) precisely because the market understands EDA to be a duopoly-grade, high-margin, recurring, mission-critical software franchise with pricing power. That peer band is the theme's *recognition* — fully formed and richly valued at the top.

The stage that matters for *this* name is the **gap between that recognition and where Silvaco trades.** SVCO is not the theme leader; it is a sub-scale specialist in the theme's adjacent, less-contested lanes — TCAD/process-simulation and semiconductor IP — plus the emerging FTCO AI digital-twin layer where the duopoly is weak. The theme is mature and capital-rich at the SNPS/CDNS apex but **early and under-recognised in its specialist tier** — exactly where a re-rating thesis lives. And the theme is *not* fading: it is mid-S-curve with T7 theme-capital still accelerating in (record CDNS/SNPS backlogs, EDA growing ~13% CAGR vs semi-R&D ~7% with the spread widening). The new demand fuel — AI-driven "virtualised process development" turning from nice-to-have into must-have — is the T7 inflection accelerating into Silvaco's specific niche, evidenced by new FTCO logos landing each quarter.
