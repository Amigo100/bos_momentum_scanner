**Stage — the re-underwrite's key shift: from early/rising to EARLY-INFLECTION-NOW-CROWDING.** The dosing-convenience re-rate is no longer nascent — APGE has published controlled data and is starting Phase 3, the marketed anchors validate the pool, and the field has taken its first reality checks (Sanofi lunsekimig AD miss, J&J NM26 AD termination, UPB −47% on a statistical asthma win).
