## 1. Macro — the regime it rides

Two regimes converge on this name, and that convergence *is* the thesis. The first is the **AI-infrastructure capex super-cycle**: hyperscaler and neocloud demand for GPU compute runs ahead of available power and data-center capacity, so the scarce input has shifted from chips to **energized megawatts** — and firms that already hold power, land, and interconnects are paid AI-economy multiples for them. The second regime is the **bitcoin engine that funds the build** — and this is where the re-underwrite finds its most material change. Capital is still flowing *into* the AI-infrastructure theme (rising capex, rising sentiment — the "early/rising" posture the system hunts), but the funding side of the dual engine is weaker than it was. The structural force on the position card is **AI Infrastructure**.
