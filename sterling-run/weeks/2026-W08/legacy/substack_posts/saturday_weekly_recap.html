Grid Infrastructure Modernization emerges as our top-scoring theme at 8.1/10, driven by AI power demand that's outpacing grid capacity by orders of magnitude. AI data centers are projected to consume 1,000 TWh globally by 2026—equivalent to adding another Germany to global electricity demand. Grid connection delays are forcing companies to pursue behind-the-meter solutions, creating immediate opportunities for infrastructure providers. Federal grid modernization funding is beginning deployment, providing additional tailwinds for companies positioned in this space. Defense Industrial Base Modernization scores 7.5/10 as the Ukraine conflict revealed critical supply chain vulnerabilities requiring years of rebuilding. This isn't about geopolitical timing—it's about industrial capacity that must be rebuilt regardless of conflict duration. Defense contractors are reporting record backlogs providing multi-year revenue visibility, while new CMMC cybersecurity requirements are creating additional barriers to entry that benefit established players. Industrial AI Infrastructure at 7.2/10 represents the less crowded way to play AI infrastructure buildout. While everyone focuses on semiconductors and software, the physical infrastructure requires massive industrial equipment—cooling systems, transformers, construction equipment. With hyperscaler capex exceeding $280B in 2026 and $400B+ in AI infrastructure construction spending, companies providing essential enabling equipment are benefiting from unprecedented demand.
