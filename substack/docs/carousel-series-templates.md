# Carousel Series Templates

> **Supplement to `carousel-guide.docx`.** Use this alongside the brand guide
> when generating carousel data JSON in claude.ai chat.
>
> The brand guide defines colours, fonts, sizing, and layout fundamentals.
> This document defines what goes ON each slide for each content series.

---

## Series Tag Mapping

Each content series uses a specific carousel series tag displayed in the
header bar on Slides 1 and 5.

| Content Series | Day | Carousel Series Tag | Slide Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| Sector Watch | Wednesday | **MACRO PULSE** | ETF flows, policy catalysts, theme positioning |
| Investor Lessons | Friday | **INVESTOR TOOLKIT** | Principles, case studies, frameworks |
| Tools & Tech | Saturday | **INVESTOR TOOLKIT** | Tool walkthrough, comparison, setup guide |
| Deep Dive | Tuesday | **BUSINESS X-RAY** | Company financials, valuation, thesis |
| The Edge | Thursday | **MARKET SIGNAL** | Counterintuitive data, system insights |

Tuesday and Thursday typically use animated diagrams rather than carousels,
but if a carousel is needed instead (e.g., a comparison piece), use the
series tags above.

## Content Density Targets

Every slide must contain minimum data points. Thin slides with one sentence
and lots of whitespace feel amateur. Rich slides with specific numbers feel
institutional.

| Slide Position | Min Data Points | What Counts as a Data Point |
|---|---|---|
| Slide 1 (Hook) | 1 headline stat | A specific number in the headline or subtitle |
| Slide 2 (Context) | 3 supporting points | Paragraphs with facts, OR 2 data citations in text |
| Slide 3 (Evidence) | 4 numbers minimum | Stat cards each with a number + label + source |
| Slide 4 (Framework) | 2 sides OR 4 items | Two comparison columns, or 4+ categorised list items |
| Slide 5 (Takeaway) | 3-4 numbered points | Actionable, specific, investment-relevant |

**BAD slide 3:** "Defence spending is rising. Institutional interest is growing. The theme is strong."
**GOOD slide 3:** Four stat cards: "$127B NATO increase" | "+34% ETF inflows YTD" | "3 of 5 positions green" | "8.4/10 theme score"

## Per-Series Slide Structures

### MACRO PULSE (Sector Watch — Wednesday)

| Slide | Background | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | DARK (Navy) | Theme name as big serif headline. One stat that proves momentum: "$800M flowed into defence ETFs this month." Date tag. "SWIPE →" CTA. |
| **2. Why Now** | LIGHT (#F5F7FA) | 2-3 paragraphs explaining the catalyst. What changed? Policy, earnings, geopolitics? One callout box with the key quote or data point. |
| **3. The Flows** | LIGHT | 4 stat cards in 2×2 grid: ETF inflow $, institutional 13F change %, theme score, portfolio exposure. Each card has Georgia 40pt number + Calibri label + Muted source. |
| **4. Positioned** | LIGHT | Two-column or list format: 3-5 stocks in this theme with current price, our position status (HOLDING / WATCHING), and one-line thesis. Highlight our holdings with green accent. |
| **5. Outlook** | DARK (Navy) | 3-4 numbered takeaways with gold badges: what to watch, key dates, risk factor, and our positioning. End with Sterling Signals URL. |

### INVESTOR TOOLKIT — Investor Lessons (Friday)

| Slide | Background | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | DARK (Navy) | Principle name as big serif headline. The surprising stat: "Druckenmiller once bet 100% of his fund on one trade. He made $1B." Series tag: INVESTOR TOOLKIT. |
| **2. The Story** | LIGHT | The case study or investor explained in plain English. 2-3 paragraphs. No jargon. Use a callout box for the key quote: "Soros: 'It's not whether you're right or wrong — it's how much you make when you're right.'" |
| **3. The Evidence** | LIGHT | 4 stat cards with the numbers that prove the principle: return %, timeframe, drawdown, comparison to benchmark. Each card sourced. |
| **4. Right vs Wrong** | LIGHT | Two-column compare: "WHAT WORKS" (green top bar) vs "WHAT FAILS" (red top bar). 3-4 bullet points per side. Practical, specific. Not abstract. |
| **5. Your Takeaway** | DARK (Navy) | 3-4 numbered action items with gold badges. Each must be something the reader can do THIS WEEK. End with portfolio connection: "We apply this with our $RCAT position — up 55% in 47 days." |

### INVESTOR TOOLKIT — Tools & Tech (Saturday)

| Slide | Background | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | DARK (Navy) | Tool name as headline. The problem it solves: "Bloomberg costs $25K/year. This does 80% of the job for free." Series tag: INVESTOR TOOLKIT. |
| **2. What It Does** | LIGHT | Plain English explanation of the tool. 2-3 paragraphs. Who it's for, what makes it different. Screenshot description of the main interface if relevant. |
| **3. Live Demo** | LIGHT | 4 stat cards showing what the tool found on our portfolio ticker. "Institutional Ownership: +16% QoQ" | "Short Interest: 8.2% of float" | "Analyst PTs: $24-$38" | "Sector Rank: #3 of 47". Real data from the tool. |
| **4. Comparison** | LIGHT | Two-column: "FREE TIER" vs "PAID ($X/mo)" listing what you get at each level. OR comparison with 2-3 alternatives: tool name, price, key differentiator. |
| **5. Get Started** | DARK (Navy) | 3-4 numbered setup steps with gold badges: "1. Go to [URL]" "2. Create free account" "3. Search your ticker" "4. Check [specific metric]". End with Sterling Signals verdict: "We use this for [specific purpose]." |

### BUSINESS X-RAY (Deep Dive — Tuesday, when carousel used)

| Slide | Background | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | DARK (Navy) | Ticker + company name as headline. One financial fact: "$42M TTM revenue, +340% YoY. Still trading at 3x forward." |
| **2. The Thesis** | LIGHT | What the company does and the structural trend it rides. 2-3 paragraphs. Callout box with the theme connection. |
| **3. The Numbers** | LIGHT | 4 stat cards: revenue, margin trajectory, institutional change, key catalyst date. Sourced from 10-Q/earnings. |
| **4. Bear vs Bull** | LIGHT | Two-column: bear case (red bar, 3 risk factors) vs bull case (green bar, 3 catalysts). Specific numbers in each. |
| **5. Our Position** | DARK (Navy) | Entry price, current P&L, base case PT, key risk, exit trigger. 3-4 gold-badged points. Footer with Sterling Signals URL. |

### MARKET SIGNAL (The Edge — Thursday, when carousel used)

| Slide | Background | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **1. Hook** | DARK (Navy) | The counterintuitive claim as headline: "The Best Portfolios Are 90% Empty." Supporting stat as subtitle. |
| **2. The Concept** | LIGHT | Plain English explanation. One paragraph. Callout box with the key research finding. |
| **3. The Proof** | LIGHT | 4 stat cards: the study results, the historical data, the market comparison. "Dalbar 2024: avg investor 5.5% vs S&P 10.2%" |
| **4. In Our Portfolio** | LIGHT | How this plays out in our real trading. Ticker, entry, P&L, what we did and why. Table or list format with green highlights on our positions. |
| **5. The Takeaway** | DARK (Navy) | 3 numbered action items the reader can do this week. Portfolio connection. Sterling Signals URL. |

## Carousel Data JSON

The carousel generator (`carousel-generator.js`) reads a JSON file following
the schema in `carousel-data-schema.json`. Key fields per slide:

```json
{
  "series_tag": "MACRO PULSE",
  "title_line1": "Defence Spending",
  "title_line2": "The $800M Monthly Flow",
  "subtitle": "Why institutional money is flooding into defence — and what it means for our positions",
  "date": "March 2026",
  "slides": [
    {
      "type": "title",
      "background": "dark"
    },
    {
      "type": "context",
      "background": "light",
      "section_title": "Why Now",
      "section_icon": "🎯",
      "paragraphs": ["...", "...", "..."],
      "callout": "Quote or key stat"
    },
    {
      "type": "stat_cards",
      "background": "light",
      "section_title": "The Evidence",
      "section_icon": "📊",
      "cards": [
        {"number": "$127B", "label": "NATO Spending Increase", "source": "NATO 2025 Report"},
        {"number": "+34%", "label": "Defence ETF Inflows YTD", "source": "ETF.com"},
        {"number": "3 of 5", "label": "Portfolio Positions Green", "source": "Sterling Signals"},
        {"number": "8.4/10", "label": "Theme Score", "source": "Weekly Screening"}
      ]
    },
    {
      "type": "two_column",
      "background": "light",
      "left_header": "RISKS",
      "left_color": "red",
      "left_items": ["..."],
      "right_header": "CATALYSTS",
      "right_color": "green",
      "right_items": ["..."]
    },
    {
      "type": "takeaway",
      "background": "dark",
      "points": [

        "2. $LMT and $RTX leading on 13F accumulation",
        "3. Our exposure: $RCAT +55%, $KTOS +12%",
        "4. Risk: de-escalation would reduce urgency"
      ]
    }
  ]
}
```

## Quality Checklist

Before generating carousel data:

- [ ] Series tag matches the content series (MACRO PULSE for Sector Watch, etc.)
- [ ] Slide 1 headline contains a specific number, not a vague claim
- [ ] Slide 3 has exactly 4 stat cards, each with number + label + source
- [ ] Slide 4 comparison uses appropriate labels (not always "AT RISK / DEFENSIBLE")
- [ ] Slide 5 takeaways are actionable (reader can do something this week)
- [ ] Every stat card number comes from research, not from memory
- [ ] Dark-light-dark sandwich structure maintained (slides 1 and 5 dark, 2-4 light)
- [ ] Total slides: 5 minimum, 7 maximum
- [ ] Brand palette used correctly: Navy headings, Gold accents, Muted labels
