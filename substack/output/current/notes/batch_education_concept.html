Seven structural forces drive our portfolio allocation. Not all of them move at the same speed.

DISCOVERY: the idea exists but no institutional capital is flowing yet. Too early for deployment. EARLY ADOPTION: smart money entering, catalysts stacking, government spending or regulatory mandates confirmed. This is where our system targets entries. CONSENSUS: everyone sees it, crowding increases, watch for exits. LATE STAGE: narrative peaks and fundamentals must carry the position alone.

Three of our current structural forces sit in EARLY ADOPTION: Defence Spending, Nuclear Renaissance, and the Biotech Capital Cycle. The system allocates capital where structural flows are confirmed but the broader market has not fully priced them.

Phase identification is the difference between buying conviction and buying a crowded trade.
