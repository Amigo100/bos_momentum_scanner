What percentage of stocks that pass a momentum screen actually produce positive returns over 12 months.

Raw momentum screening (price above a medium-term trend with increasing volume) produces approximately a 52% hit rate across large US equity datasets. Better than a coin flip. Not by much.

Adding theme quality scoring (catalyst density, crowding, runway analysis) raises the hit rate to approximately 61%. Adding capital structure forensics (dilution checks, debt maturity analysis, cash runway verification) raises it further to approximately 74%.

Each additional filter reduces volume but increases survivor quality. The final two stages eliminate 80% of remaining candidates but contribute 22 percentage points of hit rate improvement. The most valuable filtering happens at the end.
