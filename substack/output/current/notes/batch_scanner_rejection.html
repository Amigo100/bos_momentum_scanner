A 3D printing company with a defence tailwind, 23% short interest, and a direct line to Pentagon procurement dollars. Our system walked away.

45% share dilution in two years. Shares outstanding grew from 130M to 189M fully diluted. A $92M convertible note with a June 2028 put date creating a balance sheet cliff. Revenue declined 12% year over year despite cost savings. The defence segment grew 16%, but that represents only 9% of total revenue.

The momentum signal was real. The business underneath it was not.

Knowing when to say no matters more than knowing when to say yes.
