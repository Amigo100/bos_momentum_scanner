Most retail portfolios hold 30 to 50 positions. Academic research on factor investing consistently finds concentrated portfolios outperform diversified ones by 2 to 4 percentage points annually. After roughly 15 holdings, each additional position adds more correlation than protection.

Our portfolio holds 13 positions across five structural forces. The top performer is up 194% from entry. The weakest sits at -17%. Both were sized at entry based on signal strength.

That proportionality matters. A 194% gain in a 13-position portfolio moves NAV. The same gain in a 50-stock portfolio barely registers. Concentration is not recklessness when paired with a screening system that rejects 99% of candidates. Friday's scan will determine whether the portfolio stays at 13 or adds a 14th.
