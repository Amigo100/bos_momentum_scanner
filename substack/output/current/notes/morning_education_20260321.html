Retail investors are 50% more likely to sell a winning position than a losing one. Terrance Odean's study of 10,000 brokerage accounts showed this pattern persists across experience levels and account sizes.

The mechanism: profit feels fragile, so it gets locked in prematurely. Losses trigger anchoring to the entry price, delaying rational exits.

Our system eliminates both biases. Two exit criteria: a structural trend break and a capital preservation protocol. Whichever fires first closes the position. No negotiation.
