2.4% of all publicly listed US stocks accounted for the entire net wealth creation above Treasury bills between 1926 and 2023. Hendrik Bessembinder at Arizona State University published that finding. The remaining 97.6% collectively matched or underperformed risk-free rates.

For momentum investors, this reframes the exercise. The goal is not to find "good stocks." It is to be in the 2.4% and avoid the 97.6%.

Our screening system rejected 99.9% of the stocks it analysed in the latest scan: 1,279 tickers entered, 18 cleared technical filters, two were deployed. That selectivity is deliberate. Bessembinder's data suggests the base rate for any randomly selected stock adding value above Treasuries is roughly 1 in 40. Systematic filtering at 99.9% elimination rates attempts to shift that base rate meaningfully higher.

The 11 positions currently in the portfolio were selected from approximately 12,000 total candidates screened since inception.
