Despite the lack of new signals, our thematic analysis reveals compelling structural shifts that will likely drive future opportunities:

AI Power Infrastructure & Grid Modernization (PRIME, 8.6/10)

The most compelling theme remains AI power infrastructure, where physical constraints are forcing a strategic pivot from relying on public grids to developing integrated power solutions. Global data center power demand is projected to grow from 55GW to 84GW by 2028, while Microsoft and Oracle have each announced data center projects exceeding 10GW capacity.

This isn't just about building more servers—it's about rebuilding America's power grid for the AI era. The Trump administration's promise to "open up energy plants" for AI development creates a multi-year investment cycle in grid modernization, nuclear power, and energy infrastructure. Hyperscalers are investing hundreds of billions in dedicated power generation because power availability has become the primary constraint on AI expansion.

Defense Industrial Base Modernization (INVESTABLE, 7.4/10)

Geopolitical tensions are driving unprecedented defense modernization, with the Pentagon's FY2026 budget request of $961.6B plus $150B in reconciliation funds creating massive opportunities in defense industrial capacity. Critical supply chain bottlenecks in solid rocket motors, electronics, and manufacturing are being addressed through strategic investments, with over $73M in solid rocket motor supply chain contracts announced recently.

The defense reindustrialization initiative represents more than just budget increases—it's a fundamental shift toward supply chain reshoring and next-generation production technologies that will create sustained investment opportunities.

Healthcare Sector Contrarian Recovery (INVESTABLE, 7.0/10)

After 2.5 years of underperformance due to policy uncertainty and COVID impacts, healthcare is positioned for recovery as drug pricing clarity emerges and AI adoption accelerates. The sector offers defensive characteristics with AI-driven efficiency upside, making it an attractive contrarian play.

Policy headwinds around drug pricing and ACA have largely cleared, while AI adoption in healthcare is driving measurable efficiency gains. With valuations remaining attractive relative to expected returns, healthcare represents a compelling risk-adjusted opportunity.
